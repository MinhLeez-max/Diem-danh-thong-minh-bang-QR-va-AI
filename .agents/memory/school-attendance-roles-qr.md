---
name: School attendance roles and QR
description: The user-selected role model and student QR check-in flow for the school attendance app.
---

The app has admin, teacher, and student roles. Students sign in with a school account linked to their roster record; scanning an attendance-session QR automatically records that student without requiring them to type a student code. Admins manage roles and student links, and teachers only access their assigned classes.

**Why:** the user selected this workflow for the school attendance project.

**How to apply:** Preserve the role boundaries and account-linked QR flow when changing authentication, class access, or attendance behavior. Camera-based attendance remains aggregate-count only, as described in [camera-attendance-privacy.md](camera-attendance-privacy.md).
